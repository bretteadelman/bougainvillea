Why It Works

Sound is working on your mind at the same time your drip works on your body. Binaural beats guide your brain into states you can't reach by trying — slower waves, quieted rumination, real stillness. Awe-inducing sound goes further: research shows it measurably reduces negative emotions and generates feelings of humility and connectedness. The "small self" effect makes your problems feel smaller. None of this requires believing anything. Just headphones.
