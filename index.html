Why It Works

Woo woo.
(Peer-reviewed.)

Binaural beats guide your brain into states you can't reach by trying — slower waves, quieted rumination, real stillness. Awe-inducing sound goes further: research shows it measurably reduces negative emotions and generates feelings of humility and connectedness. The "small self" effect makes your problems feel smaller. Your place in things feels bigger.

None of this requires believing anything. Just headphones.

"Awe produces feelings of a diminished self and increased connection to others — alongside measurable reductions in negative affect." — Stellar & Keltner, PNAS 2015
