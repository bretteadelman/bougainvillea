What the Market Is Watching

The challenges
every operator
is navigating.

Per Solomon Partners' Q2 2024 MedSpa Market Overview, investor scrutiny of med spa platforms concentrates around five operational themes. Each one is an opportunity for Bougainvillea-equipped operators.

  1. 01

    Shifting Consumer Trends

    Definitions of beauty and wellness shift fast — often virally. 95% of customers under 40 arrive with product preferences already formed. Operators who can't shape the experience lose control of the narrative. Bougainvillea gives your room a distinctive sensory identity that clients remember and describe to others.

  2. 02

    Competitive Landscape

    9,400+ locations competing on overlapping service menus. Quality operators rise, but in a crowded market, differentiation requires more than the right equipment. The ambient experience — how your room feels — is one of the few dimensions that's still uncontested.

  3. 03

    Demand Seasonality

    Med spa revenue spikes in summer and around social events. Operators who build emotional stickiness between peak seasons — through experiences clients want to repeat, not just results they want to maintain — normalize revenue more effectively. Bougainvillea makes the session itself the reason to return.

  4. 04

    Payment Models & Loyalty

    Recurring revenue is the metric PE platforms are optimizing hardest. The operators who convert one-time visits into loyalty programs are the ones building durable businesses. An emotionally memorable experience is the most effective loyalty mechanic that doesn't require a discount.

  5. 05

    Patient Experience as a Value Driver

    Solomon Partners explicitly flags patient experience as a primary value-defining theme across med spa transactions. At $2M average annual revenue for a single-location operator, the margin between a good experience and an unforgettable one compounds significantly over time and LTV.
